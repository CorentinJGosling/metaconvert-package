!br_guess = br — no misspecification in this viewThe assumed baseline risk is exactly correct here, so the substitution cost this study exists to measure is not being exercised. Change br_guess to see it.

!br = 0.01 — sparsest cell in the gridSmallest baseline risk in the grid. At n = 50 the whole 2×2 table holds 0.31–0.75 expected cases, so the Haldane–Anscombe correction applies to 91–99% of replications (26–76% at n = 300). Much of what is compared here is the behaviour of that correction.

·Smallest mean RMSE dipietrantonj 0.903, largest vanderweele_rr_squared 1.753The other three routes fall within 0.017 of one another. These aggregates carry no Monte Carlo SE for RMSE, so that ordering cannot be separated from simulation noise — switch to bias or coverage for an error bar.

·RMSE and bias disagreevanderweele_rr_squared has the smallest mean |bias| (0.292) and the largest RMSE, because its empirical SE is about twice the others (1.696 against 0.759–0.852). Closer on average, much less dependable on any one study.

!Coverage 0.992–1.000 is over-coverage, not calibrationSE ratio 1.77–2.27 for every route, mean interval width 5.0–12.9 on the log-OR scale: intervals roughly twice as wide as they should be. The programme records this across both binary-outcome studies, names the continuity correction as one candidate, and has not chased it.
