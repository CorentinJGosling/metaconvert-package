Two questions, without the questions

Direction B revised against four notes: the study picker had to be a control, the plot options had to sit with the plot, the prose had to name estimands rather than ask rhetorical questions, and there had to be less of it.

What changed

1 the top filter was not visible The study is now the only field on the page — boxed, with the index, the title, a chevron and prev/next beside a position counter. Every other control is a token or a tick, so the one that changes everything below it is the one that looks changeable.
2 plot options at the top, plot not visible The full-height rail is gone. Axis, panels and scale now sit inside the plot section, beside the figure they redraw. Route selection moved into the routes table, where the colour key already was.
3 the sentences were not academic The two rhetorical questions are replaced by a table stating what each route estimates, with its reference: point-biserial (Cooper et al., 2019) and biserial (Jacobs & Viechtbauer, 2017).
4 too much text Body copy is down by about two thirds. The ledger and the routes table carry what four paragraphs used to.
